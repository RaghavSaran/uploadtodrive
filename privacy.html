Live Recorder · Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Applies to the Live Recorder web page at raghavsaran.github.io/uploadtodrive and the Live Recorder Android app.

What Live Recorder does

Live Recorder records video from your phone's camera in short parts (one minute by default) and uploads each part to your own Google Drive while you record. Each recording gets its own folder inside a folder called "Live Recorder" in your Drive.

Google account and Google Drive data

  • You sign in with Google and allow one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It lets the app create files and folders in your Drive and see or change only the files and folders the app created. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • With this permission the app creates the "Live Recorder" folder and one folder per recording, uploads the video parts, writes a short description on each part (recording time, part number, length, resolution and device model), and reads back the size and checksum of each uploaded part to confirm the upload is complete.
  • The app reads your Google account's email address to show which account the recordings go to.
  • Your videos travel directly from your device to Google Drive. The developer runs no server and never receives, sees or stores your videos, your Drive data or your account details.
  • Live Recorder's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the recording and upload features described here. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models.

Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only while you have the camera turned on or are recording. The Android app can keep recording with the screen off or in the background until you press Stop; Android shows a notification the whole time.

Data on your device

  • Android app: finished parts are kept in the app's private storage only until Google Drive confirms them, then deleted. If you use "Save pending", copies are saved to your phone's Movies/LiveRecorder folder.
  • Web page: parts are kept in the browser's memory until Google Drive confirms them.
  • Settings (quality, part length, the signed-in email address and the ID of the "Live Recorder" folder) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site data removes them.
  • There are no analytics, ads, trackers or crash reporters.

Third parties

Sign-in and uploads are handled by Google (Google Identity Services and the Google Drive API) under Google's privacy policy. The web page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your control and deletion

  • Your recordings stay in your Google Drive and you can delete them there at any time.
  • "Sign out" in Live Recorder's Settings removes the app's access to your Drive. You can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Because the developer stores no data about you, there is nothing for the developer to delete; removing the app's access and deleting the recordings in Drive removes everything.

Children

Live Recorder is not directed at children under 13.
